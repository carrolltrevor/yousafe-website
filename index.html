Not available yet. Field testing begins in 2027.

What YouSafe is

YouSafe is two apps. Crews use the phone app to fill in safety forms on site: field-level hazard assessments (FLHAs), vehicle and equipment inspections, incident and stop-work notifications, incident investigations and training records. The office uses the desktop app to set YouSafe up, collect the forms and file them.

The phone app works without signal. Forms wait on the phone until they can go.

Who it is for

Small contractors: the crews who fill in safety forms on site, and the people in the office who file them.
